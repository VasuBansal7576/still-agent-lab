# Sandbox refund history for replay

The replay gate requires trusted, complete evidence that the session's capture has no prior refunds. The PayPal adapter cannot establish that evidence from the documented provider responses used here. Do not create `complete: true` or `refundedAmount: '0.00'` from a missing list, a capture status, or an empty Transaction Search result. A session-bound fresh capture alone does not exclude a concurrent or external refund.

PayPal's [capture details endpoint](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/captures-get/) retrieves one capture by ID. Its [refund details endpoint](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/refunds-get/) retrieves one refund by a known refund ID. The [refund guide](https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/refund-payment) lists a separate payment and refund tracking system as a prerequisite. These sources do not establish a complete current listing of all refunds or attempts for a capture. Capture status distinguishes completed, partially refunded, and refunded states, but does not prove that there are no pending or uncertain attempts. See the [capture definition](https://developer.paypal.com/sdk/payment/v2/definitions/capture/).

[Transaction Search reporting](https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/PayPal_AG_Grid_Part_1/) can lag by up to three hours. An empty or fully paginated response immediately after capture is therefore not evidence of zero refunds. A refund total on an individual refund response also does not prove the absence of an unknown or pending refund.

The existing `getCapture`, `getRefund`, and `listAccountTransactions` adapter methods return individual sanitized provider responses. None returns a complete refund history. No server-owned history lookup is implemented because the provider contract above does not support one with the required completeness. The replay executor must continue to reject absent or incomplete history rather than accepting client-supplied history as proof.

A future implementation needs a trusted system that records every refund decision and uncertain outcome from capture creation onward, excludes or accounts for external refund paths, and reconciles provider evidence. It must bind that evidence to the session and capture, fail closed after gaps or uncertain outcomes, and prevent concurrent refund decisions. Live PayPal behavior and any proposed completeness guarantee remain unverified.
