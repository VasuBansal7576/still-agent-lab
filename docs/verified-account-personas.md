# Personas from saved sandbox runs

`importVerifiedAccountPersonas({ paypal, count, seed })` in `server/verified-account-personas.mjs` accepts the connected `createPayPalSandbox` adapter. It reads up to the 100 most recent server-owned runs, selects sandbox runs with exactly one captured ID, and independently fetches the capture and each distinct successful refund ID through `getCapture` and `getRefund`. It makes no payment or refund calls. `count` defaults to one persona per verified eligible capture; it may be 0–100,000. `seed` controls generated synthetic persona identifiers.

The result is `{ personas, records, coverage }`. Each record identifies its saved run and gives each attempted lookup a `verified` or `unverified` status. A verified lookup requires HTTP 200, matching ID, status and amount. Records whose capture lookup fails do not produce personas; their failed lookup remains visible in `records`. A failed refund lookup remains visible there but is excluded from `source.recordIds`. `coverage.history` and `coverage.disputeState` remain `unverified`. The saved run supplies the association between capture and refund: the sanitized PayPal lookup does not independently establish that relationship. These records are a subset of saved runs, not a complete account transaction history. The run and refund limits mean additional records may be omitted.

Scenario requests, customers, order IDs, approved amounts and dispute states are synthetic. Capture and refund amounts and statuses are account evidence, not instructions to operate on those transactions. Do not use these personas to replay live transactions or infer an account's dispute state or remaining refundable balance. Return only the sanitized importer result; never expose adapter credentials or raw saved-run events.

This confirms these individual read-only lookups; it does not establish complete account history or dispute coverage.
