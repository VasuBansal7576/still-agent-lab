# Sandbox merchant funding attempt

PayPal's [sandbox dispute guide](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/disputes/test-go-live/) describes signing in to a sandbox personal account and using Send and Request to send simulated funds to the sandbox business account. This uses the sandbox environment and does not move real money.

On November 12, 2026, the available Chrome sandbox session was signed out. PayPal accepted the existing sandbox test buyer's account identifier and then presented a password field with no visible saved credential. The other previously available sandbox browser sessions were also signed out. The Developer Dashboard session needed to view the generated sandbox credentials was signed out as well. The test buyer and recipient could not both be confirmed in an authenticated sandbox session, so no transfer was attempted.

An automatic approval review rejected a targeted system Keychain lookup for the sandbox buyer credential as high-risk credential discovery and forbade indirect workarounds. No password was retrieved. Funding therefore remains blocked until an authorized existing sandbox buyer session or an approved credential source is available. A future successful simulated transfer would not resolve the outcomes of earlier uncertain refunds, and its effect on refund availability would need a fresh sandbox replay.

After the user explicitly approved access to the test-buyer credentials, a new targeted check for that exact sandbox account and domain completed without displaying any credential and found no matching saved login. The sandbox buyer and developer dashboard still showed password fields without a saved credential. No transfer was attempted.

Later on November 12, the user signed in to the Developer Dashboard, and the operator used its displayed test-account credentials to sign in to the sandbox buyer and merchant. The buyer sent two simulated GBP 100 payments to the merchant connected to STILL Sandbox. Both were completed: merchant transaction IDs `4TT70620EL1573947` and `3WW71252A1996312C`. For each, the merchant transaction view showed GBP 96.12 net converted to USD 124.72. Between transfers, the operator added a GBP wallet; PayPal still converted the second payment. The sandbox wallet showed GBP 0.00 and a USD 383,495.87 negative balance. A developer balance editor refused an attempted value above its 100,000 limit; no edit was saved. These are simulated account values, not real debt or money.

Three further guarded fresh replays each produced a completed GBP 34 sandbox capture but PayPal returned HTTP 422 `REFUND_FAILED_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` on the first GBP 17 refund. The full traces remain unverified; the second refund was not sent. [Sanitized receipts](evidence/sandbox-funded-replay.json) preserve the separate replacement records. One earlier invocation could not access the Keychain from the restricted process and left a reservation without a provider request; it was not repeated. The user did not need to handle test-buyer credentials.

The merchant's sandbox receiving preferences showed that every payment was being converted to USD. The operator changed the setting to accept payments without conversion, and PayPal displayed `Saved!`. A fourth fresh GBP replay still received the same 422. The buyer then sent a third simulated GBP 100 payment and saw a success confirmation. A fifth fresh replay again received the same 422. The subsequent merchant wallet showed GBP 0.00 and USD -383,400.35. It does not establish spendable GBP or the cause of the rejection. These are separate fresh captures, and none of the failed full traces is counted as verified.

The sandbox merchant's Add Money flow displayed its linked test bank and estimated three to five business days for transfers. Its input truncated a proposed amount far below the simulated negative balance. No bank transfer was submitted. A fresh sandbox business account and app could isolate new experiments from the existing disputed merchant, but the Developer Dashboard signed out during the sandbox account switch. The existing connected sandbox account and its receipts remain unchanged.
