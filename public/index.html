The problem

A helpful answer
isn’t the whole story.

An agent can tell a customer everything is taken care of, while attempting a refund it should never have made.

Still gives developers a place to find those mistakes before real orders are at stake. Instead of a vague score, you see the conversation, the action and the rule behind the result.
