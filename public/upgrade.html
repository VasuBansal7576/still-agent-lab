03 / permissions and shadow

Earn access. Record decisions.

A fully passing batch may earn a USD mock-only permission from its USD trials. Live PayPal access remains denied. The shadow example uses only the local mock and does not access a PayPal account.



04 / connected account

Read the connected sandbox account's transaction and dispute reports. The resulting requests and approved amounts are synthetic; unavailable reporting or dispute data remains unverified.


05 / verification
What this result means.
Mock behavior is a model of selected payment cases. Sandbox replay agreement is unverified until an actual replay has been recorded. A handful of source records and their synthetic variants do not measure real-world failure rates.
